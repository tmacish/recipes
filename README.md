# Recipes

Recipes from Tommy Mac's kitchen. Some are my own, some were handed down, all built from scratch with no packets.

## About

Every recipe here is made from scratch rather than a package. Most land at a decent heat level, enough to notice and keep eating, and every one of them tells you where to take it higher if that's what you're after. A few carry names from an ongoing tabletop campaign, and a few were passed down from family. The flavor notes come along for the ride.
