# Thalen's Smoked Adobe Beef
### *(or: What the Fallen Priest calls Barbacoa)*

*Fresh-rotel edition. Same vengeance, less aluminum.*

---

## Ingredients

**For the Spice Rub (Dark as Underdark Chain Mail):**
- 2.5 to 4 lb chuck roast
- 1/2 tbsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tbsp cumin
- 1/2 tbsp onion powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 2 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp black pepper

**For the "I Keep My Promises (But Bend My Word)" Sauce:**
- 3 to 4 roma tomatoes
- 2 fresh Hatch chiles, mild pods if you want the party to survive (Anaheim if you can't find Hatch, poblano in a pinch)
- 3 to 5 dried guajillo chiles, stems and seeds removed
- Half a 7oz can chipotle peppers in adobo, about 3 to 4 peppers plus a spoon of the adobo (the whole can was atrocity even by Thalen's standards, so he shows restraint this once and resents it)
- 1 orange, juice only
- 1 lime, juice only
- 1 14.5oz can beef broth
- 1 yellow onion, sliced
- 3 bay leaves
- Kosher salt and an extra splash of lime, to taste (the fresh tomato and chile bring no built-in seasoning the way the can did, so you're making that up by hand)

**Wood:**
- Hickory. Rounder and safer than mesquite and won't turn bitter on the long cook. Swap in mesquite if you want Thalen at full atrocity.

---

## Instructions

**1. Fire Up the Smoker (Like a Forest Community Under Threat):**
Get your smoker to 250 to 275°F. Hickory this time. It burns cleaner than mesquite and won't go acrid over a long cook, which is the one mercy Thalen refuses to extend to anyone else.

**2. Season the Roast (Darker Than Elven Chain Mail):**
Mix garlic powder, cumin, onion powder, paprika, chili powder, salt, and pepper. Rub it all over the chuck roast like you're coating it in ranger-priest spite.

**3. Smoke Phase (The Long Bow Draw):**
Place the roast directly on the grate with an aluminum pan underneath to catch drippings. Smoke for ~2 hours until it hits 165°F and develops a bark darker than Thalen's moral compass.

**4. Char the Fresh Stuff (Trial by Fire):**
While the beef smokes, blister the roma tomatoes and Hatch chiles over an open flame or under the broiler until the skins are blackened all over. Drop the chiles into a covered bowl or a bag and let them steam 5 to 10 minutes, then peel, stem, and seed them. Core the tomatoes. This is your from-scratch rotel, and the char is what buys back the roasty note the can used to fake.

**5. Rehydrate the Guajillo (Patience Before Vengeance):**
Toast the guajillos in a dry pan about 30 seconds until fragrant, then soak them in hot water or a ladle of the warm beef broth for 15 to 20 minutes until soft and floppy. Keep the soaking liquid to thin the blend if it needs it.

**6. Blend the Sauce (Bend Your Word):**
Into the blender: the charred tomatoes and Hatch, the softened guajillo, the half can of chipotle in adobo, orange juice, lime juice, and a good pinch of salt. Blend until smooth and terrifying. Taste it. You want heat, smoke, and a fruity red backbone. Adjust salt and lime until it stops tasting flat, because nothing came pre-seasoned out of a can this time.

**7. Braise Phase (Slide Toward Atrocity):**
Pull the roast at 165°F, set it in the aluminum pan with its drippings, and pour the sauce over it. Add the beef broth, sliced onion, and bay leaves. Cover tightly with foil.

**8. Finish the Cook (Results-Oriented, Like Thalen's Superiors):**
Back into the smoker for another 2 to 4 hours until probe tender (~200°F, and pull it there rather than letting it climb, since past that it dries out). Rest it wrapped 20 to 30 minutes before shredding so it holds its moisture. The meat should pull apart like a broken promise to a necromancer.

**9. Serve (But Don't Call It "Shaft" Barbacoa):**
Shred the beef and serve in tacos. Pairs well with not healing your party members and keeping just enough promises to stay tolerated.

---

*Optional (keeping his promises but bending his word): toss 1 or 2 dried chile de arbol in with the guajillo for a meaner edge without wrecking the depth.*
