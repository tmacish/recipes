# Trish's Snickerdoodles

*My mom's recipe. In loving memory of Trish McDonald, a Scot who made these better than anyone, and who is missed at every batch.*

These are the real thing: cream of tartar, a proper cinnamon-sugar roll, and no shortcuts. If your kitchen smells like cinnamon and your first tray disappears before it cools, you're doing it right. Best with a good strong cup of tea, the way she'd have had it.

---

## Ingredients

- 1 cup butter, oleo, or shortening
- 1 1/2 cups sugar
- 2 eggs
- 2 3/4 cups flour
- 2 teaspoons cream of tartar
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/4 teaspoon salt

*For rolling:* cinnamon and sugar (a rough 1 tablespoon cinnamon to 1/4 cup sugar does the job)

---

## Instructions

1. Cream the butter, sugar, and eggs.
2. Add the dry ingredients and mix well.
3. Shape into walnut-sized balls and roll in the cinnamon-sugar mixture.
4. Bake at 350°F for 10 to 12 minutes.
5. Makes approximately 4 dozen.

---

## Notes

- **"Oleo"** is margarine, for anyone born after it stopped being called that. Butter makes them richer; she'd tell you to use whatever's in the fridge and not fuss about it.
- Pull them while the centers still look a touch underdone. They firm up as they cool and stay soft that way.
- The cream of tartar is not optional. It's what makes a snickerdoodle a snickerdoodle instead of a cinnamon sugar cookie.

*Made with love, and passed down the same way.*
